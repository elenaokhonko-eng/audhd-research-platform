# Privacy notice

This notice explains what the member platform at audhdneurobiology.org knows about you, why, where it is kept and how you can have it removed.

## Who is responsible

The platform is operated by **GuideBuoy AI SG Pte Ltd**, a company registered in Singapore ("we", "us"). For any question or request about your data, write to **info@guidebuoyai.sg**. That address also reaches the person responsible for data protection.

The newsletter itself is published on Substack. What Substack collects when you read or subscribe there is covered by Substack's own privacy policy, not by this notice.

## What we hold about you

| What | Where it comes from | Why we need it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your email address | You, when you sign in | To recognise you and to send the sign-in link |
| Your name and Google account identifier, if you sign in with Google | Google, with your agreement | To sign you in. We never receive your password |
| Whether a paid Substack subscription exists for your email address, its status, whether it is monthly or annual, and its start and renewal dates | The Stripe account through which Substack bills its subscribers, which we read without being able to change anything | To give you the access your subscription includes |
| Your subscription type on Substack (for example complimentary or gift) | A subscriber list the publisher exports from Substack | To recognise readers whose subscription is not billed by card |
| The versions of the terms and of this notice you accepted, and when | You | To show that you agreed |
| Which invitation link you first arrived through | The link | To count how many people each invitation reached |
| Which library files you downloaded, and when | Your use of the library | To prevent bulk copying and to know which items are useful |
| Messages you send us through "Tell us" or by email | You | To answer you |
| Technical records: the time of each request, your IP address and browser type | Your browser | To keep the service secure and to find faults |

We do not receive or store your card number or any other payment detail. Payments are handled by Substack and Stripe.

The platform does not ask for health information. If we later invite members to take part in a research survey, that survey will have its own information sheet and its own consent, and taking part will always be optional.

## A note on the exported subscriber list

The file Substack produces contains more than the platform uses, for example a reader's name, country and reading activity. The platform reads only the email address, the subscription type, the plan and the paid dates. The file is kept in a restricted storage area and is deleted automatically after seven days.

## How we keep your identity separate

Inside the platform, records about access and activity are filed under a code derived from your email address with a secret key, not under the address itself. Internal messages between parts of the system carry these codes only. Your email address is stored in two places: your member record and your contact record.

## Cookies

The platform sets one cookie, which keeps you signed in for up to 14 days. It cannot be read by scripts in the page. We use no advertising cookies and no analytics that follow you across sites.

## Who else handles your data

| Company | What it does for us | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Cloud | Runs the platform and stores its data; sends the sign-in email; signs you in | Our database and files are in Google's Singapore region. Google's sign-in service may process account data in other countries |
| Stripe | Holds the subscription records we read | Stripe's own locations |
| Substack | Publishes the newsletter and bills subscriptions | Substack's own locations |
| Cloudflare | Directs visitors to the site (domain name service) | Worldwide |

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

| What | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Your member record, contact record and access records | While you are a member |
| Download records and support messages | 12 months |
| Technical records | About 30 days |
| The exported subscriber list | 7 days |
| A record that an access right was granted, changed or ended, filed under the code and without your email address | 24 months, so that mistakes can be traced and corrected |

## Your choices

- **See or correct** what we hold: write to us.
- **Delete your account:** use "Delete my account" on your Account page, or write to us. Your sign-in stops working at once. We remove your member record, your contact record and your access records within **30 days**. Deleting your platform account does not cancel a Substack subscription; you do that in your Substack account.
- **Withdraw your agreement** to this notice: this is done by deleting your account.
- **Complain:** you may contact the Personal Data Protection Commission of Singapore, or the data protection authority where you live. We would like the chance to put things right first.

## Age

The platform is for adults. You must be 18 or older to register.

## Changes to this notice

Each version has a date. When we change the notice in a way that matters, the platform asks you to read and accept the new version before you continue.
